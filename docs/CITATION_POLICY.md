# Citation Verification Policy

No reference is final merely because it appears in a search result, a secondary bibliography, or a citation manager.

For every manuscript reference, record the following before final use:

1. Full bibliographic metadata from the primary standard body, publisher, or proceedings record.
2. DOI or a stable official URL.
3. The exact section, page, table, or figure supporting the cited claim.
4. The claim wording used in the manuscript.
5. Verification date and reviewer initials.

Use standards bodies for normative or standardization claims, publisher records and full texts for research findings, and vendor documentation only for vendor-specific implementation facts.

The active evidence register is maintained in
[`citations/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`](citations/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md). It is updated
before, not after, any source is introduced into manuscript prose.
